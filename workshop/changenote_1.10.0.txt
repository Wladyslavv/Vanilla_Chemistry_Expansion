1.10.0: New machine: the HPLC (sold by the Cartel Vendor while you have none, 1000). It splits a filtered sample into four collection slots by polarity, every solute having a polarity modelled on its real counterpart; it runs on methanol of 99% or better and uses 3-9 mL of it per mL of solute. Its polarity table scrolls and sorts by name, polarity or slot, and it comes with a manual. Methanol: a hydrogen canister heated to 150°C or more on an upgraded heating plate turns 3 parts of hydrogen into 1 part of methanol vapour, which condenses into pure methanol when the canister is dragged onto a liquid container (let it cool first). Disinfectant tablets: a gas canister now goes straight into the Chem Finisher, which presses 100 mL of its chlorine into a vanilla box of water purification tablets. Mislabelled antibiotic tablets and beer now count as fraud like other products. New mod settings: Recoverable filter residue (on by default) and Advanced Kotton fabric nightly repair (1000 by default). Another setting labels blended wine from the Chem Finisher genuine by default (off by default). An opened nuclear waste barrel now holds 3 L instead of 1.5 L.
