[h2]Vanilla Chemistry Expansion[/h2]
Built on the vanilla chemistry system (extraction, heating, refrigeration, filtering, evaporation, pressing); vanilla products and reactions are unchanged.

[h3]Dream Toxin (handbook chapter 1)[/h3]
A legal medical outlet for Dream Caps and Nightmare Caps.
[list][*]Mix the two extracts 1:1 with a little acid and chill it in a fridge; burn off leftover extract, filter, dry and press [b]Dream Toxin Injectors[/b].
[*]Popular with Upper Level cosmetic clinics; a legal medical item, base value 150. Only pharma grade (90%+) pays: about street Dream Dust money, without the notoriety.[/list]
[h3]Brine electrolysis (new, handbook chapter 10)[/h3]
A simplified chlor-alkali process: 1 part salt + 0.3 part water ends up as 1 part base and 1 part acid, both free of chemical contaminant and heavy metal.

[h3]Hollow's Lantern and Incapacitant (handbook chapter 9)[/h3]
A lab-bred chili, and the legal riot-control agent refined from it.
[list][*][b]Hollow's Lantern[/b]: annual, innate Capsaicin Production III gene, spliceable into other crops. The retired farmer brings two seeds each visit.
[*]Blend for capsaicin, add at least 4x the alcohol and heat to 50-78°C for Lantern Ester (too little alcohol leaves physical contaminant); filter, evaporate the alcohol and press [b]A Small Vial of Incapacitant[/b].
[*][b]Wiping your record[/b]: each vial sold to the Upper Level or Security takes price × purity × 5 off your crimes; once those are cleared, every full 100 left takes 1 off investigation progress.[/list]
[h3]Xinke and Crackine (handbook chapter 8)[/h3]
A new crop, and the new ice refined from the alkaloid in its leaves. No medical tag: raw leaves earn less than Dream Dust, refined Crackine a little more.
[list][*][b]Xinke[/b]: perennial, drought-tolerant, no replanting after picking. Innate Alkaloid Production gene, spliceable into other crops. The retired farmer brings seeds; the shady farmer sells leaves.
[*]Blend the leaves, remove the protein, add acid, boil dry, drive off leftover acid in the evaporator and press [b]Crackine[/b] (a small vial ≈ 1.5 Dream Dust; a large one = four small).
[*][b]Regulars[/b]: a personal-use buyer who gets 90%+ Crackine comes back the next day; each daily visit after that adds 25% to their budget (up to 2000), with special lines.[/list]
[h3]Ore chemistry: purifying acid and base (handbook chapter 2)[/h3]
No filter removes the chemical contaminant and heavy metal in vanilla acid and base, which keeps drugs from lab-pure grade.
[list][*]Crush metal ore in a [b]blender with all 4 chem modules[/b] for calcite and quartz sand.
[*][b]Purifying base[/b]: calcine calcite into quicklime and add it to base to remove the chemical contaminant; heavy metal settles out on its own. Filter.
[*][b]Purifying acid[/b]: quartz sand and base make water glass; add acid to precipitate silica gel. Heated silica gel drives the contaminant out of acid; freezing removes heavy metal. Filter.
[*]Needs an upgraded heating plate.[/list]
[h3]Starch genes[/h3]
Nutrifruit's starch and sugar now come from innate Starch and Sugar Production genes: same totals, spliceable into other crops.

[h3]Industrial alcohol (handbook chapter 3)[/h3]
[list][*]Blend nutrifruit, hydrolyse the starch into sugar with acid and heat, neutralize, add yeast: alcohol by morning. Each yeast tolerates a different strength.
[*]Boil off the methanol; freeze and filter out ice to concentrate to about 25%.
[*]The chem scanner shows a container's temperature; mod settings adjust boiling, fermentation and fridge cooling speed.[/list]
[h3]Blended wine (handbook chapters 3 and 4)[/h3]
[list][*]Dry-distil plant matter into acetic acid, esterify it with alcohol into ethyl acetate, then press it with alcohol and water into wine.
[*]A labeller sets two labels: quality and method (genuine/blended). Lying is risky: unnoticed sales add up to fraud, and Upper Level clients refuse blended wine.[/list]
[h3]Beer (handbook chapters 5 and 6)[/h3]
[list][*]The Chem Finisher fills weak nutrifruit brew into a 2 L [b]Beer Keg[/b], graded premium, ordinary or undrinkable; dilute with good or pure water for premium.
[*]Double-click the keg to pour one: a [b]can of beer[/b] at room temperature, [b]draft beer[/b] once chilled. Draft sells best but goes flat overnight.
[*]Beer counts as water: water buyers take it too.[/list]
[h3]Advanced Kotton fabric[/h3]
A gold Kotton fabric that regains full durability every day, found wherever the vanilla advanced water filter is.

[h3]Nuclear waste reprocessing (handbook chapter 7)[/h3]
[list][*]Pry a barrel open with a crowbar, dissolve the spent fuel in strong acid, add reducing agent, extract the plutonium with ethyl acetate, boil dry and fill [b]Plutonium Ampoules[/b].
[*]An ampoule dragged onto a drink makes a Nuclear drink worth +50; only Upper Level clients and addicts buy it.
[*]Vitrify the leftover fission products with water glass.
[*][b]Radiation sickness[/b]: nuclear waste left unprocessed overnight lowers your selling prices; genuine Immunivax eases it.
[*]A Waste Broker comes every 7 days from day 7, selling nuclear waste.[/list]
