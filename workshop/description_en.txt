[h2]Vanilla Chemistry Expansion[/h2]Built on the vanilla chemistry system (extraction, heating, refrigeration, filtering, evaporation, pressing); vanilla products and reactions are unchanged.
[h3]Machines[/h3][list][*][b]HPLC[/b]: splits the solutes of a sample into 4 collection slots by polarity. The mobile phase must be methanol of 99% or better, and each mL of solute separated uses 1-3 mL of it.
[*][b]Condenser[/b]: drag it onto a heating plate to make a still. What boils off goes into a 1 L vapour chamber and is condensed into a collecting container by coil (overnight), evaporative (uses coolant) or closed-loop (uses battery power) cooling.
[*][b]Separatory Funnel[/b]: splits an oil phase from an aqueous phase and drains them apart.
[*][b]Electrolyzer[/b]: brine into base, hydrogen and chlorine; fat into toluene; with an empty feed slot, nitric oxide from the air.
[*][b]Fermenter[/b]: grows Acremonium on feed; each round yields four strains of the next generation.
[*][b]Reloading Press[/b]: presses pistol rounds from nitrocellulose, non-lethal, standard or armour-piercing; armour-piercing also uses TNT.[/list]
[h3]Products[/h3][list][*][b]Dream Toxin Injector[/b] (the beauty shot): a legal medical product of Dream Caps, bought by Upper Level cosmetic clinics.
[*][b]Crackine[/b] (small and large vial): new ice refined from Xinke leaves; not medical, and it brings a regular back the next day.
[*][b]Incapacitant[/b]: a riot-control agent refined from Hollow's Lantern; selling it to the Upper Level or Security wipes out crimes.
[*][b]Antibiotic tablet[/b]: pressed from cephalosporin C, sellable from pharma grade; also cures sick livestock in the same pen.
[*][b]Bundle of Dynamite[/b]: a weapon and contraband pressed from TNT. Sell two or more a day to the Lower Level or the Revolution and a price rise follows the next day.
[*][b]Blended wine[/b]: pressed from ethyl acetate, alcohol and water; its labels can be changed with the labeler.
[*][b]Beer Keg[/b]: pours canned beer at room temperature, or draft beer once chilled; draft beer goes flat overnight.
[*][b]Plutonium Ampoule[/b]: turns a drink into a Nuclear drink worth +50, or makes a Plutonium Chemical Battery recharge itself every night.
[*][b]Disinfectant tablets[/b]: 100 mL of chlorine pressed in the Chem Finisher into a vanilla box of water purification tablets.[/list]
[h3]Tools[/h3][list][*][b]Gas Canister[/b]: holds hydrogen, chlorine, hydrogen chloride, nitric oxide and methanol vapour. On a heating plate it makes hydrogen chloride or methanol vapour; dragged onto a container, its gases dissolve into acid or condense into methanol.
[*][b]Plutonium Chemical Battery[/b]: a 200 kJ battery that recharges itself every night once given plutonium ampoules.
[*][b]Advanced Kotton Fabric[/b]: restores its durability every night; how much is set in the mod settings.
[*][b]Opened Nuclear Waste[/b] and [b]Vitrified Waste[/b]: where nuclear waste reprocessing starts and ends.
[*][b]Advanced Chemistry Handbook[/b]: notes on every process, sold by the Cartel Vendor.[/list]
[h3]Crops and materials[/h3][list][*][b]Xinke Shrub[/b]: a perennial whose leaves hold an alkaloid.
[*][b]Hollow's Lantern[/b]: a lab-bred chili full of capsaicin.
[*][b]Acremonium strain[/b]: blended for cephalosporin C; frozen, it can be copied without limit.
[*][b]Rock Salt Ore[/b]: ordered from GP Mining, crushed for salt.
[*][b]Nitrocellulose[/b]: nitric acid poured on Kotton fiber; the propellant for rounds.
[*]New liquids: methanol, acetic acid, ethyl acetate, toluene, nitric acid, TNT, salt, alkaloid, capsaicin, cephalosporin C, plutonium, water glass, silica gel and more.[/list]
[h3]Characters[/h3][list][*][b]Cartel Vendor[/b]: from day 5, every 7 days; sells this mod's machines, handbook, batteries, strains, seeds and gas canisters.
[*][b]Waste Broker[/b]: from day 7, every 7 days; sells nuclear waste.
[*]The cartel network's contacts, traders, couriers and Security officers (cartel expansion, off by default).[/list]
