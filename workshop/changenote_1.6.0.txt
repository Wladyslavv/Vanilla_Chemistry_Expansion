1.6.0: New brine electrolysis line. Order rock salt from GP Mining (6 or 12) and crush it in any blender into salt. The Electrolyzer (sold by Dr. Jackson, with two empty gas canisters and instructions) electrolyses brine each night into base, hydrogen and chlorine. Gas canisters hold 5 L, merge by dragging and vent with right-click; heat hydrogen + chlorine past 120 C on a heating plate to make hydrogen chloride, then drag it onto a container with water for acid up to 40%. The retired chemist sometimes sells empty canisters; the handbook gets a Brine Electrolysis Notes chapter. Xinke's innate Alkaloid Production goes from II to III (seeds in existing saves are upgraded once).
