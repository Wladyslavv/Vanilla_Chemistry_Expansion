1.11.0: New machine: the condenser (sold by the Cartel Vendor while you have none, 600). Drag it onto a heating plate to make a still: whatever boils off the container on the plate goes into a 1 L vapour chamber (first in, first out) and is condensed into a collecting container in one of three modes: coil (once a night, 1 mL per 8 mL of coolant), evaporative (gradually, using up 4 mL of coolant per mL) or closed loop (gradually, 5 mL per kJ from a battery). Pure water is the best coolant: its effect scales with the square of its water purity. Right-click the still to take the condenser off again. New machine: the separatory funnel (two on every Cartel Vendor visit, 200 each): it splits an oil phase (fat, toluene, ethyl acetate, Lantern Ester) from an aqueous phase and drains the lower layer up to or past the interface. New reactions: nitric acid and base neutralise 1:1; protein chars into insoluble debris at 155°C or more; nitric acid now boils off at 120.5°C, above any nitration. A powered fridge cools gas canisters. The Reloading Press presses 1, 5 or 10 rounds at a time and sounds like a pistol being reloaded. The HPLC now uses 1-3 mL of methanol per mL of solute (was 3-9). The Water boiling speed setting is now Liquid boiling speed and applies to every liquid that boils. Once the 12-unit orders unlock, Caulson's phone menu splits metal ore and rock salt into their own submenus, so no choice drops off the menu. Redrawn from slightly above like the vanilla machines: fermenter, electrolyzer and gas canisters.
