1.11.1: New tool: the Flow Hose (sold by the Cartel Vendor, 150). Drop it on a liquid container, then on a second one: the first then flows steadily into the second, 1-100 mL a second (default 5). Right-click it and choose Configure to set the flow and its conditions: flow only while the receiving container is under X% or X mL, only while the source holds over X% or X mL, and a limit on how much one link moves. Right-click either linked container and choose Detach to take the hose back. New look: 53 item and machine sprites redrawn by xXStrayXx, among them the fermenter, electrolyzer, HPLC, reloading press, separatory funnel, gas canisters, plutonium battery, nuclear waste, crops and products; the liquid in the separatory funnel now fits the new glass. The condenser and still hold 5 L of coolant (was 2 L); ones you already have are enlarged on load. The Cartel Vendor now comes every 7 days only once you have the Retired Chemist (bought from Joe Wild, or the Chemist start), otherwise every 28 days; his first visit is still on day 5. While you have no Advanced Chemistry Handbook it is always the first item on his counter, so it is never crowded off.
