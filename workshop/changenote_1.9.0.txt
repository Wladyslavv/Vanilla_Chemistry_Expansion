1.9.0: Explosives. The Electrolyzer gets an on/off switch on its feed panel (off by default); switched on with nothing in the feed slot, it makes nitric oxide, which dissolves in water into nitric acid. Caffeine turns into fat at 150°C, and with a liquid container in product A the Electrolyzer turns fat into toluene. Toluene and nitric acid (1:3) with at least 10% acid as catalyst nitrate into trinitrotoluene at 50°C or more; the Chem Finisher presses 250 mL of it into a Bundle of Dynamite (weapon, high-level contraband). Selling two or more bundles to the Lower Levels or the Revolution in one day always sets off one of the vanilla price-rise events the next day. Nitric acid dragged onto Kotton fiber turns it into nitrocellulose, the propellant for the new Reloading Press (sold by the Cartel Vendor), which presses .22 and heavy pistol rounds, non-lethal, normal or armour-piercing; armour-piercing rounds also take trinitrotoluene. The handbook has a new Explosives Production Notes chapter with its own bookmark. Acetic acid now neutralizes with base, after the vanilla acid. Fixed gas canisters looking empty until they were moved. New textures for the opened nuclear waste barrel, vitrified waste, beer can and mugs, Dream Toxin injector and antibiotic tablets. Incapacitant is no longer bought by every Upper Level and Security client, only by those who shop for medical goods or weapons. Fixed the plutonium chemical battery showing a placeholder picture in the recharger. / 1.9.0：火药工业。电解机的原料面板新增开关（默认关闭）；打开开关且原料栏为空时，电解机会产出一氧化氮，一氧化氮溶于水生成硝酸。咖啡因在 150°C 以上会变成油脂；产物 A 放液体容器时，电解机会把油脂电解成甲苯。甲苯与硝酸按 1:3 混合，加入占总量 10% 以上的酸作催化剂，加热到 50°C 以上生成三硝基甲苯；化学制剂成型机用 250 毫升三硝基甲苯压成一捆炸药（武器，高危违禁品）。同一天向下层区或革命军售出两捆以上炸药，第二天必定触发一个原版的涨价事件。把硝酸拖到韧棉纤维上可以制成硝酸纤维素，作为新机器子弹复装机（卡特尔小贩出售）的发射药，可压制 .22 手枪弹和重型手枪弹的非致命弹、普通弹、穿甲弹；穿甲弹还要消耗三硝基甲苯。手册新增《炸药制取笔记》一章及其书签。乙酸现在可以与碱中和（碱先中和原版的酸）。修复气罐在拖动前显示为空的问题。更换了打开的核废料桶、玻璃固化体、罐装啤酒和啤酒杯、梦魔菌毒素注射剂、抗生素片的贴图。失能剂不再被所有上层区和治安部顾客购买，只有采购医疗品或武器的顾客才会买。修复钚化学电池放进充电器后显示为占位图的问题。
